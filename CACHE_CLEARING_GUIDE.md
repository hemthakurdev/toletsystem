## 🌐 **Browser Cache Clearing**

To see the button styling fixes, you need to clear your browser cache:

### **Chrome/Edge**
1. Press `Ctrl+Shift+R` (Windows) or `Cmd+Shift+R` (Mac) for hard refresh
2. Or press `F12` → Right-click refresh button → "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"

### **Firefox**
1. Press `Ctrl+F5` (Windows) or `Cmd+Shift+R` (Mac) for hard refresh
2. Or press `F12` → Right-click refresh button → "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"

### **Safari**
1. Press `Cmd+Option+R` for hard refresh
2. Or go to Develop menu → "Empty Caches"

### **Alternative Method**
1. Open Developer Tools (`F12`)
2. Right-click on the refresh button
3. Select "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"
